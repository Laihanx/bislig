Festivals & Celebrations

Experience Bislig City's vibrant culture through dance, music, and faith-filled traditions.

Citywide Highlights

Mark your calendar for the biggest cultural events and signature celebrations in Bislig.

Tinuy-an Festival — September

A month-long celebration culminating in the vibrant "Sayaw sa Tinuy-an" street dance showcasing the city's natural bounty. Expect float parades, agro-trade fairs, and eco-tour promotions.

Barangay Fiestas

Community gatherings honoring patron saints with processions, culinary spreads, and cultural shows.

Poblacion Fiesta

📍 Poblacion Zone I 🗓 May 15 🙏 Patron Saint: San Isidro Labrador

Street dancing, agro fairs, and novena masses highlighting agricultural heritage.

Mangagoy Fiesta

📍 Barangay Mangagoy 🗓 July 27 🙏 Patron Saint: Sr. Santiago

Fluvial parade along the harbor, marching bands, and seafood cook-off competitions.

San Vicente Fiesta

📍 Barangay San Vicente 🗓 August 28 🙏 Patron Saint: St. Augustine

Highlights include cultural night, bamboo raft race, and community thanksgiving meal.

San Jose Fiesta

📍 Barangay San Jose 🗓 March 19 🙏 Patron Saint: St. Joseph

Fisherfolk regatta, coastal clean-up drives, and blessing of boats tradition.

Coleto Fiesta

📍 Barangay Coleto 🗓 November 24 🙏 Patron Saint: Christ the King

Community banquet, choral competitions, and lantern-lit procession at nightfall.

Burboanan Fiesta

📍 Barangay Burboanan 🗓 January 20 🙏 Patron Saint: Sto. Niño

Vibrant child-focused celebrations, traditional games, and cultural storytelling.

Festival Reminders

  • Confirm exact schedules with the local tourism office as dates may adjust yearly.
  • Book accommodations early during peak festival weeks.
  • Respect local customs and join community clean-up efforts after events.

Make the most of every celebration.

Pair festival visits with nearby attractions and dining recommendations for a memorable trip.

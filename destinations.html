Tourist Destinations

Explore cascading waterfalls, island escapes, and eco-tour experiences that define Bislig City.

Signature Spots

Plan your itinerary around these crowd favorites and hidden treasures across Bislig.

Tinuy-an Falls

The three-tiered "Niagara Falls of the Philippines" with misty rainbows at sunrise.

📍 Barangay Borboanan ⭐ 4.9

Hagonoy Island

A postcard-worthy sandbar with powdery white sand and vibrant turquoise waters.

📍 Lianga Bay ⭐ 4.8

Hinayagan Cave

Marvel at cathedral-like chambers, stalactites, and shafts of light in this underground wonder.

📍 Barangay San Vicente ⭐ 4.7

Lawigan Beach

Unwind on a peaceful shoreline surrounded by lush greenery and gentle Pacific swells.

📍 Barangay Lawigan ⭐ 4.5

Bislig Baywalk

Catch sunrise over the harbor, stroll with locals, and enjoy pop-up weekend food stalls.

📍 Bislig City Proper ⭐ 4.6

Lake 77 Eco Park

Kayak and picnic in a serene reservoir surrounded by forested hills and cool breeze.

📍 Barangay San Jose ⭐ 4.4

Insider Tip

Schedule your visit to Tinuy-an Falls between 7:00 AM and 9:00 AM to witness vivid rainbows. Arrange boats to Hagonoy Island in advance, especially on weekends and holidays.

Ready for an island-hopping adventure?

Combine the falls, caves, and beach tours with a weekend stay for the full Bislig experience.
